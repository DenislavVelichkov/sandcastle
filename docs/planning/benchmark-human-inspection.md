## Decisions

The user selected the existing judge score with a human inspection guide, five task-derived summary items, the existing cost-versus-score graph, no saved human review results and unchanged project acceptance.

The percentage remains judge-derived specification adherence. The controller calculates earned weight divided by assessed applicable weight, multiplied by 100. A met verdict earns the full frozen weight, a partial verdict earns that weight times the frozen partial-credit fraction, and a not-met verdict earns zero. Not-assessed requirements stay outside that score denominator but remain inside applicable weight for coverage and whole-rubric uncertainty bounds. Only frozen task applicability excludes a criterion. No assessed weight means no numeric score.

Coverage remains assessed weight divided by all applicable weight. Counts of met, partial, not-met, pending and inapplicable requirements are supporting information, not a new percentage. Stale assessments have no current score or current checklist claim. Required check failures remain explicit even when the judge score is positive.

The five numbered human inspection steps cover:

1. Read the exact frozen task and its full rubric, including requirement text, weights, partial credit, applicability and required evidence.
2. Inspect the generated candidate source and patch against the task's code criteria and cited file/line ranges.
3. Inspect the frozen configured check and its retained log against criteria requiring check evidence.
4. Inspect available runtime captures, receipts and frozen references against applicable visual criteria. A task with no frozen runtime requirement says so; required missing evidence remains unavailable.
5. Inspect unmet, partial and pending judge findings, coverage and comparison limits before making a separate human/project decision.

Each step includes the selected task's relevant criteria and retained evidence. These are inspection actions, not five equally weighted requirements or recorded human verdicts. Comparable arms share the same task criteria. Candidate-specific evidence and outcomes remain distinct, including linked retries. No checkboxes record human sign-off.
